Website hosting

The portfolio is hosted through GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you access pages or images, your browser connects to GitHub infrastructure. GitHub may process technical connection data, including IP addresses, to deliver the site and maintain security. GitHub's own privacy information applies to its processing.

Optional visitor email notifications

Visitor notifications are optional and disabled until I complete setup. If enabled, no notification request is sent unless you actively select “Allow visit notification” in the preference prompt. Refusing has no effect on your ability to access this website.

If you agree, your browser sends a short request to a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare can infer your full IP address, estimated city/region/country, and network owner from your network connection. We may also process the browser user-agent, page path, time, and referring website domain. That information may be forwarded through a protected Google Apps Script receiver to my Gmail address as a visitor notification.

The network owner does not necessarily represent your employer, and the location may be wrong or imprecise. No attempt is made to identify named individuals through this data.

Legal basis: your optional consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where applicable, § 25 TDDDG. Declining means no optional visit notification is sent. The notification service does not use marketing pixels or advertising cookies.

To remember your selection, the website stores a small preference value in your browser's local storage. After consent, it also stores a session-only notification flag to avoid sending repeat alerts from refreshes. Those values stay in your browser and do not contain your IP address.

How to change your choice

When optional visitor notifications are enabled, use the “Privacy choices” control in the portfolio footer to withdraw consent or change your decision. You can also clear the website's local storage in your browser settings. Withdrawing consent stops future notifications; it does not automatically delete older emails.

Retention and recipients

Consent-based alerts are intended only for short-term interest in portfolio visits. I aim to delete visitor emails containing IP addresses within 30 days, subject to setup of an appropriate retention routine. Until then, deletion is manual. Data recipients for optional alerts are Cloudflare, Google Apps Script and Gmail. Their services may process data in countries outside the EEA. Appropriate transfer safeguards and their privacy terms should be reviewed before activation.

Other third-party connections

Some project preview thumbnails are requested from the public WordPress.com mShots screenshot service. Opening a project, GitHub or LinkedIn link will connect you to that external website. Your browser and those services may exchange standard technical request data, including your IP address, under those providers’ respective privacy terms.

Your rights

Subject to applicable legal conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing. You can withdraw consent for optional visitor notifications at any time. For requests about this site, email hjsarker@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority.

This notice describes the planned technical implementation. The site's owner should review its completeness, international transfers, retention practice and any required legal imprint before enabling optional IP-address notifications.
